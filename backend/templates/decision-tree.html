Diagnosis Step 1
Does the arc start at all?
Does the arc immediately go out after starting?
Is the power switch ON and the correct outlet voltage selected?
Likely cause
Wire speed too low or contact tip clogged. Increase WFS by 10% and inspect the contact tip — replace if worn or spattered. Also check drive roll tension isn't too loose. (Owner's Manual, p. 24)
Arc is stable
The arc itself is fine. Your issue is likely in post-weld appearance — check gas flow rate (15-25 CFH), work angle, and travel speed. If you're seeing porosity or spatter, ask a more specific question.
Check connections
Ground clamp connection is the most common cause. Make sure it's clamped directly to clean bare metal — not paint, rust, or the workbench. Also check output terminals are tight. (Owner's Manual, p. 12)
Power setup issue
Check power first. Confirm the switch is ON, the voltage selector matches your outlet (120V vs 240V), and the circuit breaker hasn't tripped. The OmniPro 220 needs a dedicated 20A breaker on 120V or 50A on 240V. (Owner's Manual, p. 8)
